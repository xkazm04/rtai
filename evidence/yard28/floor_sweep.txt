Bars: patrol blue >= 65 % outside and inside the pools (hue 200..250 kept: 221 / 223 at every value), rifle >= 95 %, pellet >= 90 %.
Retention is each cop against his own daylight on the same floor, so a darker floor does not hurt it (their daylight contrast falls with the
floor, e.g. outside day 4.92 -> 4.32 at 61, but dusk falls with it). The bar that stops the floor is Rivet's luminance delta: he is the dark
figure (body L* 30.7) on the floor, a darker floor closes the gap, 0.19 sits at floor L* ~60.6.

DARKEST FLOOR THAT PASSES (modelled): L* 61 between pools, C* 24, hue 66 (sRGB ~177,140,109), set in arched_line as
ground[0].tint ffe0c8ff and ground[1].wash f6c18480 (stains tint and ground_shade 0.81 unchanged: the gain is per channel, shade is a flat multiply,
so tint/wash is the right knob and a pure shade change would not move the hue). This is 4 L* darker than today and 11 deg redder / C* +11 more
saturated: it matches the plate's mean (L* 59.9, C* 24.5, hue 65.7) in hue and chroma and stops 1 L* short of its lightness because of the Rivet bar.
The red channel cannot be raised by a tint (it is already ff), so the real floor will land a little below the modelled 177 red: expect L* 60-61.
