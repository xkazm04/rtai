# Tonnage p55 profile shoe tilt, 2026-11-19

The p55 C3 raised outer rim inclines 34° on the right profile and 36° on the left, in opposite directions. Some derived shoes were level throughout that handoff. The tables compare each visible raised rim with the interpolated angle of the same rim in its two neighbouring shipped keys. Positive angles slope down to screen right. Errors are target minus measured angle, in degrees.

The measurement fits the same facing’s C2 planted shoe in two-degree increments at four scales. It searches a 17 × 11 px neighbourhood of each tracked pin. The C4 cropped rim uses only the visible columns. The fit excludes a silver foot within 5 source px of the lowest support and any rim occluded in either source. M11 and M19 are double support. The 2° search grid limits angular precision.
