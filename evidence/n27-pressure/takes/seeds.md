The caption "Hits 4" of the first film counted every grazing blast, not rockets on target.
- Pair damage split (`PressurePair`): each rocket round's own `landed` signal reports where it burst; if HP fell in that tick it is `direct` when the burst point lies inside Rivet's body radius (the sim then deals the blast at full strength, falloff 1) and `splash` otherwise (the blast reaches him at reduced strength). `other` is HP lost with no rocket burst (0 in every seed); `hp_lost` is the sum of all three. The split sums to his HP lost exactly.
- Method: `project/dev/gauntlet/pressure_seeds.gd` (node tools/dev/run.mjs pressure_seeds -- --out=<dir>), `PressureTake.open(id, seed)` then `advance()` to `length`, headless, per seed one fresh street. `PressureFates` watches the arena's shot list after each tick for the per-round fate columns of the sword. Raw rows, with the first 12 round fates of every 6 m take (`fate_log`), are in `seeds.json`.

## Why the plasma sword catches about half of the rounds fired at it (blade6)

Measured over 10 seeds, 1300 rounds fired: 650 (exactly half, 65 per seed) arrive with no deflect at all and hit Rivet, 334 are turned back and 316 get through the 50 % pass roll of an active blade (a pass is `DeflectRule.pass_ratio` 0.5 and also hits Rivet); none missed. All 650 unswung rounds arrived while the blade was mid-swing: the shipped sword runs in `single_swing` mode (`melee_weapons.json`, `single_swing: true`), one sweep of windup 8 + active 6 + recovery 19 = 33 ticks, and `MeleeCombo.press` ignores presses until that recovery ends; the deflect only acts in the 6 active ticks (`PlayerMelee.step` calls `PlayerDeflect.bat` only in `Phase.ACTIVE`). The `blade6` salvo (`PressureRifles.near(true)`: 36-tick beat, 2 rounds, `gap_ticks` 18) puts the second round of every pair 18 ticks after the first, inside the 33-tick swing that the bot started for the first round (the `fate_log` of seed 1 shows rounds 0, 2, 4 arriving in a swing started for them and rounds 1, 3, 5 arriving with swing index 1 already running). The next pair starts 36 ticks after the first, after the 33-tick swing has ended, so the first round of each beat is always swung at and the second never. So the half-catch is the take's cadence meeting the sword's 33-tick commitment (18-tick spacing < 33), not a flaw in the deflect rule: of the swung rounds, 51.4 % (334 / 650) return and 48.6 % pass, close to the 0.5 pass ratio. The share of returned rounds that hit a shooter (27.2 %) is unaffected by which rounds are swung at.

## Why `pair_dash` dashes 13 times for 26 rockets

The two troopers fire in volleys of two rockets one tick apart (launch ticks 81, 82, 195, 196, 309, 310, ... in seed 1), every 114 ticks. The bot profile asks for a dash 16 ticks after each launch, so each volley asks for two dashes one tick apart (ticks 97 and 98); the second press lands inside the first dash (10 ticks, then a 40-tick cooldown) and does nothing. One dash per volley: 13 volleys in 1500 ticks, 26 presses, 13 dashes (`dash_presses` and `dashes` in `seeds.json`).
