## Design (keep it; deviations go in your report)
- New `project/boot/prologue/prologue_span_shadow.gd` (Node2D, typed, < 150 lines), created by the over-print attach (or `prologue_game.gd`, one line) as a child drawn on the over-print layer AFTER the spans and BEFORE the actors (so it falls on the beam, under Rivet's body).
- "On a span" = Rivet's feet point (`stage.arena.rivet.pos`, metres) falls on an opaque pixel of a span's art (alpha >= 0.5) after the piece's footprint/rotation/crop transform: build the span hit masks once (Image alpha of each span texture with the same transform `PrologueYardPaint.wreck` uses), not a bounding rect (the spans curve).
- Shadow: a soft dark ellipse under the feet (about 1.1 x 0.45 m at Rivet's x1.5 visual size; a radial gradient texture made once, no per-frame allocation), ink colour at peak alpha about 0.45 (tune by look), fade in and out linearly over 6 ticks counted on the fixed 60 Hz tick (never frame delta; read the position, never write it). Visual only: no sim, RNG, collision or replay change.
- One data value so it can be switched off: optional `arched_line` key `"span_shadow": {"alpha": 0.45, "fade_ticks": 6, "size_m": [1.1, 0.45]}` (absent = no shadow), with the schema property and parsing in `prologue_yard_dressing.gd`.
