- **Sweep**: it picks the lane toward Rivet, then sweeps its aim across an arc (about 50 degrees, from one side of Rivet to the other, at a fixed angular speed) while firing bursts of **small** rounds along a **visible aim line** (the same aim-line machinery the other gunners use; a sweep that is drawn before and while it fires). A Rivet who stands still in the lane is hit; one who moves out of the lane, behind a solid prop, or (after guard's merge) guards, is not.
