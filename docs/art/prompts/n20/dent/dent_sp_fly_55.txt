Image 1 is the character: EJECTED, the ghost of a crash-test dummy robot thrown through a windscreen, exactly as the big hero drawing in image 1: only the body from the belt up; a round dummy head with one big black-and-yellow quartered disc on the side of the head and a wide black visor with a white star crack; a black-and-yellow quartered disc on each shoulder; dented riveted chest plates with a small steel plate on the chest; a dark diagonal seatbelt strap across the chest to a dark waist belt with a square steel buckle; two jointed dummy arms with segmented forearms and blocky four-finger hands; worn steel and rust specks. His whole body is a luminous glowing TEAL (#2EC4B6) spectre with the black ink linework clearly visible through the glow, pale aqua highlights on the top edges and deeper teal in the shadows; the discs stay black and yellow, the belt stays dark. Below the waist belt the body tears off into a short ragged flame-shaped plume of teal light, no longer than his head is tall, with three or four small pale glass shards in it. NO LEGS, NO FEET and NO HIPS in any drawing. Image 2 shows the ink, the outline weight, the fill, the figure size and the grid layout of our sprite sheets: match its style and its 55-degree high three-quarter camera (the top of the head AND its front both clearly visible), NOT its character (image 2's tan dummy with legs is a different character). Image 3 is a camera guide only, never drawn. Draw a GRID of FOUR ROWS and FIVE COLUMNS on a flat solid magenta (#FF00FF) background, one size for every figure, well separated so nothing crosses into a neighbour: column 1 facing the camera, column 2 three-quarter toward the camera facing picture right, column 3 right profile, column 4 three-quarter away facing picture right, column 5 straight away (back to us). Every pose leans along the direction its column faces: toward the camera means DOWN the picture, three-quarter means along the diagonal, profile means straight to the side, away means UP the picture. Row 1 HOVER: floating upright with a slight forward lean, both arms hanging loose and a little apart, the hands open, the plume hanging below him. Row 2 DIVE: thrown forward at full speed, the torso tipped forward nearly flat along the facing, the head leading, both arms swept back along his sides, the plume streaming straight back behind him. Row 3 SWING: whipped round a hard turn like a rag doll, the torso rolled onto its side, the head lolling, both arms flung loose and wide, the plume swinging out the other way. Row 4 HIT: jolted back by a blow from the front, the head snapped back, the chest arched, both arms thrown forward and up with the hands spread, the plume bunched under him. No text, no numbers, no grid lines, no slot borders, no motion lines, no ground, no shadow, no glow on the background.
