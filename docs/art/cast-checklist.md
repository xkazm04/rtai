# Wave character art acceptance

Use the shipped patrol cop as the drawing and scale reference. Judge the enemy that actually spawns in the wave, with its weapon and all its attachments. The patrol itself has gaps, particularly its firing tell. The [cast audit](cast-audit.md) records those gaps.

Measurements are from the RGBA files in `project/features/characters/art/view/{p70,p55}/`. Dimensions below are **one cell**, width × height in source pixels. The game grid has five facing columns. The generation sheets put facings in rows. Run `python tools/art/cast_metrics.py` to reproduce dimensions, navy values, contour samples, key-colour counts and exact-file provenance matches.

## Wave admission

A character enters a wave only after the equipped in-game character passes every applicable item in this table. Record each result for the archetype in the cast audit. Measure the native viewport at both pitches and both review scales.

## Checks to sign off

| Item | How to check | Patrol reference |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Compare the equipped figure against the owner's chosen plate at 32 and 64 px/m, front and back. Identify it from body, head and equipment silhouettes with captions covered. Check idle, moving, hurt and downed for costume or weapon swaps. | Navy cap, face and drawn pistol in the equipped patrol. |
| Walk | Play the two opposing contacts and passes through a whole loop for each facing. Compare unarmed and equipped states when both occur in play. A fixed emplacement should remain bolted while its own turret turns. | Four drawings per facing. |
| Attack and tell | Film from before the first harmful tick through recovery. Identify the first readable anticipation, the active pose and the end pose, and match each to the sim. The patrol holds its aim silhouette and supplies no separate anticipation frames in the ordinary wave. New threats need an identifiable pre-impact cue. For the lancer the four requested poses are shoulder, lock aim, discharge and recover. | Zero dedicated pistol anticipation frames. The new threat must show its own cue. |
| Hit | Hit a living actor from different octants. Check for recoil around a planted foot or anchored mount, silhouette and equipment continuity and return to idle. Compare the head/torso scale with the previous walk frame using `tools/art/street_hit_size.py`. | 0.20 s flinch, 0.34 s stagger. |
| Death | Step from the killing blow to rest at each pitch. Check buckle, descent, final resting silhouette, direction of the blow and equipment. A mechanical collapse or power-down should retain the machine's identity. Inspect the rest frame after the death clip stops. | Five poses at 70 degrees, three at 55. Prone length 121.3 px at 64 px/m in the paired 70-degree still. |
| Facings | Supply down, down-right, right, up-right, up in that order. The renderer mirrors down-left, left and up-left about the foot anchor. Check all eight ordinals using `RigHeadCard` and the loadout in profile. Avoid asymmetrical details that make the mirrored weapon appear on the ground or detached. | Down, down-right, right, up-right, up. Mirror the other three. |
| Grid | Walk input has 5 rows × 4 separate figures. `tools/art/street_walk.py` transposes to 5 columns × 4 rows, 192 px cell height. Cell width follows the widest figure and its drift. Keep the full weapon inside its cell. Use 5 × 3 raw for a base fall and the wound job's 5+3 raw layout for hit. Run the appropriate cutter, inspect every cell including diagonals, then check the runtime grid division. | Five columns, four armed walk rows. Cells 173 x 192 and 136 x 192 px. |
| Pitches | Render independently at 55° and 70°. Compare helmet top, shoulder overlap, weapon foreshortening, line of sight and the fall hand-off. Both pitches have native patrol locomotion and fall. The basic living wound reuses p70 in the shipped reference. Flipping supplies left facings, never a second pitch. | Native 70 and 55 degree walks and falls. Basic hit uses 70. |
| Scale | Rivet is 1.65 m (52.8 px at 32 px/m before his visual multiplier of 1.5). `StreetWalkerCards` uses 1.70 m at p70 (54.4 px) and a 2.0 m drawing correction at p55 (64 px). Crowd ID varies body scale by up to 16%. Armed fill is 1.12 / 1.08 at 70° / 55°. Compare the same facing, ID, camera and idle pose. The measured reference footprints in height are Rivet 77 / 75 px and patrol 43 / 45 px. Record the inclusive body-and-gear height separately from designed stature. | 1.70 m design height. 43 / 45 px equipped at 32 px/m (70 / 55). |
| Ink | At the shipped 192 px cell height, trace the front passing patrol pose's left contour. From the first alpha ≥128 pixel to the first pixel with max RGB ≥48 at y=70,75,80 the runs are 3,3,4 px (70°) and 3,3,3 px (55°). A comparable primary outline reads at about one screen pixel on a small human. Inspect at native 32 and 64 px/m for closed clean contours, lost hands or duplicate internal hatching. | 3, 3, 4 px at 70 degrees and 3, 3, 3 px at 55. |
| Palette | Sample the blue fabric with the `street_tone.py` mask (HSV hue 190–250°, saturation >.2, value >.08, alpha >.5). Measured mean HSV of patrol armed walk is 221.9°/.470/.330 at 70° and 221.4°/.439/.335 at 55°. Standing means are 223.1°/.472/.325 and 222.3°/.442/.328. Compare fabric with its own still under neutral light. Cops wear blue. Use ink, steel, skin and subdued local uniform colours as appropriate. Red is blood only, vermilion is reserved for danger, objectives and Rivet's collar, teal for souls and amber for heat. Retain the owner's specified amber/black equipment hazard bands. | Armed navy mean HSV 221.9/.470/.330 and 221.4/.439/.335. |
| Cut | Start with a uniform key, e.g. #00B140. The art library's `tools/sheet.py prep` and `grade` use `sheetlib.key` (median of four 8 × 8 corners, tolerance 14% of the RGB diagonal, soft transition to 28%, hard green test, nearby spill suppression with a 7 px max filter). For 5+3 wounds use `ghost_cells.py` and the hit-cell cutter. Where needed use `despill_cells.py` for magenta and `street_armband.py` for a spurious band. Tone the packed walk with `street_tone.py`. Inspect silhouette holes, interior skin and muzzle on dark and light mattes. At alpha ≥128, the patrol's two armed walks have zero green-leading or magenta-leading pixels at a 50-level channel difference. | Zero strongly green- or magenta-leading opaque pixels in either armed walk. |
| Feet (planted) | Lock the camera and mark the ground. Track the midpoint of the support sole in the lowest visible outline band (bottom 5% of the figure, alpha >128, following `tools/film/foot_slide.py`) in screen pixels. Follow the same boot on every rendered frame from touchdown until lift, including drawing changes. Measure the greatest distance between any two sole positions in that contact. Test right profile and down-right at both 70° and 55°, at the walk and run speeds used by gameplay. Cite the speed source. If there is no run, record that and test walk only. Identify any unmeasurable facing and explain why. A fixed turret base must stay still through its sweep. | At 32 px/m every mobile contact must stay within 2 screen px (0.0625 m). The largest body jump between consecutive rendered frames must also stay within 12 screen px (0.375 m) at every gameplay speed. |
| Shadow | Use a separate ground contact. In shipped combo `RigCastShadows` uses cast_k=.35, radius=.5 × hip height, y flatten=.62 and down-sun offset=.05 × hip height. Rings have radius shares 1/.78/.55 and alphas .22/.24/.30. Toggle the shadow in a same-pose exposure and check the feet after the shot and during a wound. Suppress baked key-sheet shadows. The lineup takes both exposures. | 236 changed pixels in the 32 px/m, 70-degree paired exposure. |
| Provenance | Compute SHA-256 on each final shipped PNG. Find its exact repo-relative path and matching digest in `steam/ai-provenance/*.jsonl`. Check tool, model, date, prompt reference, references, seed, human edits, acceptance and shipping scope against the ledger README. Patrol p70 still, walk, armed walk and generic hit match November lines 1454, 1729, 1704 and 1996. P55 armed walk matches 1721. The current p70 between-fall and patrol's shoulder/gut files lack exact matches. Keep the round, input plate, keyed source and cutter edits with each new delivery. | Armed walks have exact November SHA-256 matches. Current between-fall, shoulder and gut need matching entries. |
